# EcoPulse — Executive Summary & Value Proposition

**One-liner:** Fitbit for your carbon footprint, with an AI coaching staff that works while you sleep.

## Problem

Carbon trackers fail because they demand tedious manual logging. Users churn within days.

## Solution

EcoPulse inverts the model: users provide low-friction signals (a photo of a meal, a utility bill PDF, a commute description), and a **multi-agent system built on Google ADK + Gemini 3.5** converts them into quantified emissions, personalized reduction plans, and predictive "what-if" simulations.

## Why Agentic AI changes the game

- **Autonomous data gathering** — the *Ingest Agent* parses receipts, bills, and photos (Gemini multimodal) instead of asking users to fill forms.
- **Personalized nudges** — the *Coach Agent* learns behavioral patterns and proposes the single highest-impact action per week ("Swapping 2 beef meals saves more than your entire recycling effort — here's why").
- **Predictive insight** — the *Forecast Agent* projects the user's 12-month trajectory and simulates interventions in real time, visualized as a living 3D "carbon globe."

## Target demo narrative (90 seconds)

1. Judge photographs their lunch → CO₂e appears on the globe in <3 seconds.
2. Coach Agent surfaces one high-leverage weekly nudge.
3. Parallel-You Simulator shows two globes diverging over 12 months.
4. Judge asks "Why was March so bad?" → grounded, cited answer from their own data.
